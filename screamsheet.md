## What is a Screamsheet?

A **Screamsheet** is custom, one-page morning newspaper delivered directly to your email inbox every morning. 

Instead of waking up and immediately falling into the digital rabbit hole of algorithmic feeds, social media, and infinite scrolling, Screamsheets give you and your family a tangible, printable summary of what you actually care about - ready for the breakfast table.

### What Can You Get?

* **Sports Scores & Standings:** Complete game results from yesterday, division tables, and blow-by-blow game summaries (MLB, NHL, NFL, and more).
* **Targeted News Summaries:** Concise, high-signal briefs without clickbait or rage-bait.
* **Ready to Print or Read:** Formatted crisp and clean as a single-page PDF that fits on standard 8.5x11 paper.
