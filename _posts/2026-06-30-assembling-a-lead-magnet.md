A "lead magnet" is some kind of gift you give people for signing up.

Like the "$5 for signing up with Draft Kings!!" you're supposed to bet with.

Or, the worm the fish gets to eat for the price of getting hooked and cooked.

Or, the peanut butter the mouse gets to lick before getting smashed in the mouse trap.

You get the idea.

So I'm building a lead magnet for Distracted Fortune!

The way it works is, I'm going to pick 3-5 articles from my blog, the ones I think are most important for new subscribers, and offer them as a nice PDF in return for joining my email list.  I'll make those specific articles go dark on the website, so you can only get them by signing up.

Don't worry, I'll send the PDF to you first, so you can print it up for your coffee table.

Below, I'll list a few of the contenders, along with a quick summary of each.  If you would be so kind, please leave a comment below with the articles YOU would like to have gotten in an introductory PDF.  What I think is useful may not be what you think is useful.

Here we go!

1. [Why You Wait Until the Last Minute, and How to Get Back to Work](https://distractedfortune.com/why-you-wait-until-the-last-minute-and-how-to-get-back-to-work/)

This article gives a clear heuristic that describes the difference between the normal human mind, versus that of someone with ADHD.  In short, it takes more effort for an ADHD mind to get engaged in something than a normal mind.  But it takes less to get hyperfocused.  Finally, it gives some strategies to those attention-deficient among us to cope.

2. [Growth Mindset versus Influencer: Defeat the Beast to Succeed](https://distractedfortune.com/growth-mindset-versus-influencer-defeat-the-beast-to-succeed/)

This article explains a lesser known, but far more powerful, definition of "Growth Mindset."  In short, how unsuccessful people take failure and blame themselves, while successful people take the same failure and learn from it.

3. [Unlock Your Subconscious: The Power of Morning Pages](https://distractedfortune.com/unlock-your-subconscious-the-power-of-morning-pages/)

I think writing Morning Pages is one of the most powerful tools in the arsenal of someone trying to be successful.  It helps you hit the day with a prepared, clear mind, ready to take on anything.

4. [Beast, Rider, and Marshall:  How to Get Control of Yourself](https://distractedfortune.com/beast-rider-and-marshall-how-to-get-control-of-yourself/)

We are each three beings in one.  The Beast is the autopilot, eating the second helping you didn't need.  The Rider has the reins of that beast, and tries to guide him in the right direction.  The Marshall is the planner, who decides what direction is right.

5. [Slaughter the Friction before Friction kills the Action](https://distractedfortune.com/slaughter-the-friction-before-friction-kills-the-action/)

Sometimes, you just can't get to the DOING part of a task.  In that case, you need to find the points of friction between now and the DOING, and see if they can't just be removed.

6. [Why Planning Less May Be a Better Plan](https://distractedfortune.com/why-planning-less-may-be-a-better-plan/)

"Fail Fast, Fail Often" is the title of a book, but also a strategy of success. When you fail, you learn more than if you sit and plan forever.  Successful people try things and fail all the time.

7. [Burn the Ships: How to Force Yourself to Risk Success](https://distractedfortune.com/burn-the-ships-how-to-force-yourself-to-risk-success/)

Sometimes you need a nudge to get yourself to do the right thing.  That nudge may be to remove retreat as an option.  This article goes through how to identify when this is a good strategy for you.

8. [Beyond Logic:  How to Listen to your Little Voice](https://distractedfortune.com/beyond-logic-how-to-listen-to-your-little-voice/)

We all have a "little voice".  It's not the one that barks criticisms from inside your head all day.  It's the one most of us rarely listen to, but almost always gives good advice.  This article helps you tune your listening in to that quiet, good one.

Those are my best articles, at least the ones I tend to reference most.  Once I finalize the list of 3-5 of these for the lead magnet, I'll get them off the website, and only offer them as a PDF for new subscribers.
