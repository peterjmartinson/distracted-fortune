_The following is a guest article by my son Isaac.  He loves baseball, and knows more about the game than anybody._

The 2020's are full of great hitters and pitchers, which means a lot of strikeouts and runs.

Let me name the great hitters:  Aaron Judge, Shohei Ohtani, Juan Soto, Mookie Betts, Ronald Acuña Jr., Fernando Tatis Jr., Kyle Schwarber, Bobby Witt Jr.  All these hitters hit great, but also strike out a lot.

These days you could be a great hitter with 37 homers and 200 strikeouts and a 260 batting average.  Today, no one seems to care how much you strike out if you hit a lot of extra base hits.

Schwarber and the Phillies are an example.  I am a Philadelphia fan, so I see the Phillies play a lot.  They hit a lot of home runs, but strike out a lot.

There are a lot of teams in the major leagues that have a lot of power, and a lot of strikeouts.

Another reason is because the pitchers, including Skenes, Skubal, Webb, Sanchez, Crochet, Peralta, King.  These Pitchers strike out a lot of batters because they swing for the fences.  Not the gap.
